Risk Register
Financial Risks
Critical No LLC — Personal Asset Exposure
Operating as a sole proprietor with paying customers creates unlimited personal liability. A billing dispute, data breach, or contract claim has no LLC shield. Every day of operation without LLC is unacceptable risk escalation.
FixFile LLC in your state (Wyoming or Delaware recommended for single-member). ~$50-200 one-time. Open Mercury business checking same day. EIN is free from IRS (online, 10 min). Timeline: 3 days of actual work.
Critical No ToS / Privacy Policy Before Revenue
Collecting payments without a Terms of Service and Privacy Policy is a legal and Stripe compliance risk. Stripe's acceptable use policy requires ToS be presented at checkout. GDPR/CCPA requirements for data collection also apply. This is not optional at go-live.
FixUse a generator (Termly, Iubenda, or legal counsel). Deploy at /terms and /privacy. Gate checkout flow to require acceptance. Sprint item exists — execute it before flipping reg_open.
High Stripe Not Configured — Revenue Blocked
All Stripe env vars (STRIPE_SECRET_KEY, STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET, STRIPE_PRICE_PLAYER) are unset in Supabase secrets. The webhook is not registered in Stripe dashboard. PRICE_TO_PLAN map will be empty at runtime. No transaction can complete. Source: stripe-webhook/index.ts:3-19.
FixPost-LLC: (1) Create Stripe account with EIN. (2) Create "TOURney Player Account" $2/mo recurring product — copy price ID. (3) supabase secrets set STRIPE_SECRET_KEY=... STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET=... STRIPE_PRICE_PLAYER=.... (4) Register webhook endpoint in Stripe dashboard. (5) Test with Stripe CLI in test mode. (6) Flip reg_open → true.
High No Accounting Tooling — Tax Reconstruction Risk
Business expenses (Anthropic ~$55/mo, domain, etc.) are not tracked in any accounting system. Year-end tax reconstruction from credit card statements is error-prone and misses deduction categories. No quarterly estimated tax payments are documented for self-employment income.
FixOpen Wave (free) or QuickBooks Self-Employed ($15/mo). Backfill 2026 YTD expenses. Set calendar reminder for quarterly estimated tax payment (June 15, Sept 15, Jan 15). All SaaS infrastructure costs are deductible as business expenses.
Medium Supabase Free Tier Limits — Surprise Upgrade Cost
Two Supabase projects (prod + staging) on free tier. Free tier pauses projects after 1 week inactivity, has 500MB DB limit, and 2GB bandwidth. At launch with active users, auto-pause is a service outage risk. Upgrade to Pro ($25/mo each = $50/mo) would double the current burn rate.
FixDisable auto-pause on prod project now (free setting). Budget Supabase Pro ($25/mo) into financial model for launch milestone. Consider dropping staging to one project to reduce cost exposure.
Low Single Price Tier — Revenue Ceiling
lib/tier-config.js only has free/pro. The 5-8-26 review referenced STARTER/PRO/CLUB tiers. A single $2/mo flat price limits ARPU expansion. Tournament organizer (admin) pricing is not modeled at all — organizers likely have higher willingness-to-pay than players.
FixNot urgent at 0 MRR. Design organizer-tier pricing (e.g., $10-20/mo per active tournament) before scaling beyond Bova. The DB schema supports it; tier-config.js needs extension.
