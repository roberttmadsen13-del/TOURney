What TOURney Is

Multi-tenant golf tournament SaaS PWA. Groups of golfers use it before, during, and after a tournament — organizers manage from an admin dashboard, players get a branded installable PWA with realtime scoring and push notifications. v1 tenant: Bova Invitational (annual golf trip). Platform hub at /platform tracks artifact lifecycle, grades, and ops. Architecture: vanilla MPA (11 tournament pages + 7 platform pages), Supabase backend, Vercel static hosting, service worker PWA.
