Install Like a Native App
Players add it to their home screen in one tap — no App Store, no download, no friction. Works offline on the course and syncs when signal returns.

Live Leaderboard
Real-time hole-by-hole scoring. Every player sees every score update as it happens. Scramble, stroke play, best ball, stableford — mix formats per round.
🎨
100% Your Brand
Your logo, your colors, your tournament name on every screen. Players never see TOURney — they see your event. Looks like a custom build. Costs like nothing.

Push Notifications
Hole-in-one alerts. Scoring open. Tee time reminders. Tournament announcements. Blast messages to every player's phone instantly.

Player Registration
Built-in registration form, handicap tiers, team assignment, player directory. Your players sign up, you manage the list — all in your admin panel.

Multi-Day Events
Up to 7 days. Different format each day if you want. You control when scoring opens and closes for each round — all from your phone.
