Format Values — Ground Truth
Canonical scoring format values · source: create.html
strokeStroke Playdefault›
Every player counts every stroke on every hole. Add them all up. Lowest total score for the round wins. The standard format most golfers know.
scrambleScrambleteam · shared ball›
All teammates tee off, then the team picks the best shot. Everyone picks up and plays their next shot from that spot. Repeat until holed. One shared team score per hole. Great for mixed-skill groups — the best player carries the team on each shot.
best_ballBest Ballteam · own ball›
Each player plays their own ball the entire hole, start to finish. The team's score for that hole is simply the lowest (best) individual score among all teammates. Everyone keeps playing — only the best score counts.
shamblesShambleshybrid · own ball from best drive›
All teammates tee off, then the team picks the best drive. From that spot, everyone plays their own ball independently into the hole. The team's score is the best individual score from there. A hybrid of scramble (on the tee) and best ball (into the green).
stablefordStablefordpoints vs par›
Points-based format scored against par. Standard scale: Double bogey or worse = 0 pts · Bogey = 1 pt · Par = 2 pts · Birdie = 3 pts · Eagle = 4 pts · Albatross = 5 pts. Highest total points wins. Bad holes hurt less than in stroke play — a blowup hole costs you at most 2 pts.
mod_stablefordModified Stablefordaggressive points scale›
Same concept as Stableford but with a more aggressive points scale that rewards birdies and eagles heavily while penalizing bogeys. Common scale: Double bogey = −3 · Bogey = −1 · Par = 0 · Birdie = +2 · Eagle = +5 · Albatross = +8. Check event rules for the exact scale being used.
matchMatch Playhole by hole›
You compete hole-by-hole against an opponent, not on total score. Win a hole = go 1 up. Tie a hole = halved, no change. The match ends when one player leads by more holes than remain. Final result is expressed as "3&2" (3 holes up with 2 left to play). Total strokes don't matter — only who wins each hole.
alt_shotAlternate Shotteam · partners alternate›
Two-player teams share one ball, taking turns hitting it. One partner tees off on odd-numbered holes, the other on even. After the tee shot, partners alternate hitting until the ball is holed. If Partner A putts out on hole 3, Partner B tees off on hole 4. Requires tight communication and trust.
chapmanChapman / Pinehurstteam · swap then alternate›
Both partners tee off, then swap — you play your partner's drive and they play yours. After those second shots, the team picks the better ball and finishes the hole in alternate shot from there. Combines individual accountability on the second shot with teamwork to close it out.
greensomesGreensomesteam · best drive then alternate›
Both partners tee off, then pick the best drive. From there, the team plays alternate shot all the way in — the player whose drive was not chosen hits the second shot. Similar to Chapman but without swapping tee shots first.
nassauNassauside bet overlay›
A wager format that splits the round into three separate match play competitions: front 9, back 9, and full 18 holes. Each segment is worth its own bet. You can win 0, 1, 2, or all 3. Often layered on top of stroke play — the main scoring format still runs, Nassau is the side action.
skinsSkinsside bet overlay›
Each hole is worth a "skin" (a point or dollar amount). The player with the lowest score on a hole wins that skin outright. If two or more players tie, the skin carries over and adds to the next hole's pot. Ties keep rolling until someone wins clean. Can be played alongside any main format.
superballSuperballteam · scramble variant›
Scramble with a designated "super ball" player per team. On pre-set holes, the super ball player's tee shot must be used — no matter where it lands. Adds pressure and fun for stronger players who get the super ball designation.
texas_scrambleTexas Scrambleteam · scramble variant›
Standard scramble with one twist: each player's tee shot must be used a minimum number of times during the round (typically 3 of 18). Prevents one great player from dominating every tee shot and forces teams to use everyone.
florida_scrambleFlorida Scrambleteam · scramble variant›
Scramble where the player whose shot was selected sits out the next shot. If your tee shot is chosen, you don't hit the second shot — your partner(s) play it. Encourages every player to contribute and adds strategy to shot selection.
ambroseAmbroseteam · scramble + handicap›
Team scramble with handicap applied. After the round, deduct the team's combined handicap allowance (sum of individual handicap indexes divided by player count) from the gross scramble score. Net score is what counts on the leaderboard.
bogey_parBogey/Par Competitionany structure · vs par›
You play against par, not opponents directly. Win a hole = net score better than par. Halve = net par. Lose = bogey or worse. Running total of holes won minus holes lost. Often played with handicap strokes to level the field.
four_ball_matchFour-Ball Match Playpairs · match play›
Each player plays their own ball throughout the hole. The lower of the two partner scores is the pair's score for that hole. The two pairs compete match-play style — best ball of pair A vs best ball of pair B, hole by hole. Classic Ryder Cup format.
waltzWaltzteam · cycling count›
A cycling team format. Hole 1: only the best 1 score counts. Hole 2: best 2 scores count. Hole 3: best 3 count. Hole 4: back to best 1. Pattern repeats across 18 holes. At some point every player's score matters — nobody can coast.
vegasVegasteam · combined score›
Two-player teams combine their scores as a 2-digit number. Lower score = tens digit, higher = units. Example: scores of 4 and 5 combine to 45. Your team's 45 competes against the other team's number per hole — lower combined number wins the hole.
quotaQuotaindividual · handicap target›
Each player has a personal point target based on handicap (typically 36 − handicap index). You earn Stableford points each hole. The winner is whoever finishes furthest above their own target — this levels the field across all handicap levels in a single competition.
bingo_bango_bongoBingo Bango Bongoindividual · 3 pts per hole›
Three points are up for grabs on every hole. Bingo = first player to get their ball on the green. Bango = player closest to the pin once all balls are on the green. Bongo = first player to hole out. Slow play is penalized — you must play in order. High handicappers can compete.
wolfWolfindividual · 4 players only›
Exactly 4 players. A "wolf" is designated each hole in rotation (last to tee off). After each tee shot, the wolf can claim that player as their partner (2v2). After all 4 have teed, if unclaimed the wolf can pick anyone — or go solo (1v3) for double points. Wolf team wins the hole = 1 pt each. Solo wolf wins = 2 pts. Solo wolf loses = opponents each get 1 pt.
snakeSnakeindividual · penalty carry›
The player who makes the most recent 3-putt "holds the snake." They carry a penalty until someone else 3-putts and takes the snake from them. Whoever holds the snake at the end of the round takes the final penalty. Adds side pressure on every putt.
eclecticEclecticany structure · multi-round›
Your score is built from your best individual score on each hole across all rounds of the tournament. Shot a 3 on hole 7 in round 1 and a 4 in round 2? Your eclectic score on hole 7 is 3. A running best-of compilation that rewards consistency across the whole event.
peoriaPeoriaindividual · post-round handicap›
A post-round handicap system for non-handicapped players. After all scores are submitted, 6 holes are secretly pre-selected (2 par-3s, 2 par-4s, 2 par-5s). Your handicap is computed from your scores on those 6 holes and applied to your gross. Net scores determine the winner.
callawayCallawayindividual · post-round handicap›
Post-round handicap system using the Callaway table. Based on your gross score, a certain number of your worst hole scores are deducted — higher gross means more holes deducted. Designed for one-time events and non-handicapped players. Net score determines the winner.
