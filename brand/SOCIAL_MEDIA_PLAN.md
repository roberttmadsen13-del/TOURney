## Strategic Context

TOURney is pre-scale. We have one live tenant (Bova Invitational), a working product, a premium marketing page, and zero social presence. Social media at this stage is not about reach — it's about proof. Proof the platform is real, proof it looks good, proof other organizers should pay attention.

**Primary goal for first 90 days:** Build an audience of tournament organizers who recognize the brand when Jordan calls them.

---

## Platform Strategy

### Instagram — PRIMARY CHANNEL

**Why:** Golf tournament organizers are on Instagram. They post event photos. They follow golf courses, golf brands, golf influencers. The visual format suits a product that looks this good.

**Audience on this platform:** Tournament directors, club social chairs, corporate outing planners. Ages 30–55. Mobile-first. Respond to polished aesthetics + social proof.

**Content mix:**
- 50% Product screenshots / UI showcases (leaderboard, scorecard, PWA install)
- 25% Social proof (event recaps, organizer testimonials once available)
- 25% Educational / positioning (pain points, "did you know" style)

**Cadence:** 3–4 posts/week. Stories 5x/week.

**Format priority:** Reels > Carousels > Static posts

---

### LinkedIn — SECONDARY CHANNEL

**Why:** Corporate outing organizers, charity tournament directors, and club managers are on LinkedIn. B2B purchase decisions get researched here. Jordan works warm leads from here.

**Audience on this platform:** Corporate event coordinators, club GMs, non-profit fundraising directors. Decision-makers, not just users.

**Content mix:**
- 40% Positioning / thought leadership ("The spreadsheet era of golf tournament management is over")
- 30% Product features framed as business value
- 20% Social proof + case studies (Bova as V1 case study)
- 10% Behind-the-scenes / company story

**Cadence:** 2–3 posts/week.

**Format priority:** Text posts > Carousels > Video

---

### X / Twitter — TERTIARY / MONITOR

Low priority for now. Golf Twitter exists but is audience-not-buyer. Monitor for brand mentions and golf-adjacent conversations. Post when there's something worth saying.

**Cadence:** As needed. No minimum.

---

### TikTok — FUTURE STATE

Not now. The organizer persona isn't here yet. Revisit when we have video content and bandwidth.

## Content Pillars

### Pillar 1 — The Product (30%)
Show what it looks like. The UI is a differentiator.

- Leaderboard screenshots mid-event
- PWA install flow screen recording
- Admin panel from a phone
- Before/after: spreadsheet vs. TOURney

**Sample caption:**
> Your players are checking the leaderboard between holes. You're watching from the cart without touching anything.
> That's what running a tournament on TOURney looks like.
> #GolfTournament #GolfOrganizer #TOURney

---

### Pillar 2 — The Pain Point (25%)
Name the thing organizers hate. We are the answer to it.

- "If you've ever built a bracket in a spreadsheet, you know."
- "Your tournament deserves better than a Google Form."
- "Golf Genius pricing for a charity scramble doesn't make sense."

**Sample caption:**
> You spend months organizing this event.
> Your players open a link that looks like it was built in 2009.
>
> There's a better way. Starts at $3/month.
> #GolfEvent #GolfTournament #TournamentDirector

---

### Pillar 3 — Social Proof (25%)
Real events. Real organizers. Real players who installed the app.

- Event recap posts with organizer permission (Bova as first case study)
- Screenshots of player registration numbers
- "X players installed the app" stat posts
- Organizer quotes once we have them

**Sample caption:**
> The Bova Invitational ran on TOURney last season.
> 40 players. 2 rounds. One organizer with a phone and no spreadsheet in sight.
>
> We're accepting new tournaments for 2026. Link in bio.
> #GolfEvent #GolfTournament #CharityGolf

---

### Pillar 4 — Brand Voice / Personality (20%)
Short, punchy. Lets the brand's dry wit show. Builds follows before they're buyers.

- "Less than a sleeve of balls per month."
- "The scoreboard your players will screenshot."
- "We made the golf tournament app you wish you had at the last event."

**Sample caption:**
> PWA. No app store. No update prompts. Players just install it.
>
> The organizer gets all the credit. We don't mind.
> #GolfApp #TOURney

## Hashtag Strategy

### Always Use
`#GolfTournament` `#TOURney` `#GolfEvent`

### Rotate By Content
- Organizer-targeted: `#TournamentDirector` `#GolfOrganizer` `#EventPlanning`
- Golf community: `#GolfLife` `#GolfCourse` `#GolfLeague` `#CharityGolf`
- Product: `#GolfApp` `#GolfTech` `#PWA`

### Never Use
Broad hashtags that attract zero qualified audience: `#Golf` `#Sports` `#App`

## Content Calendar — First 30 Days

### Week 1 — Launch Presence
- Post 1 (IG): Product reveal. Leaderboard screenshot. "This is what a golf tournament looks like in 2026."
- Post 2 (LI): Brand intro. "We built the tournament platform we wish existed." Rob's voice.
- Post 3 (IG): Pain point. Spreadsheet vs. app. No caption needed.
- Story series: "How it works" — 4 frames, swipe through

### Week 2 — Show the Product
- Post 1 (IG): PWA install flow. "Players add it to their home screen. No app store."
- Post 2 (LI): "The admin panel that runs from your phone." Feature-as-benefit.
- Post 3 (IG): Pricing anchor. "Starts at $3/month. Less than a sleeve of balls."
- Story: Poll — "How do you run your tournament today? Spreadsheet / Other app / I'm embarrassed to say"

### Week 3 — Social Proof
- Post 1 (IG): Bova Invitational case study. Real event, real numbers.
- Post 2 (LI): Same, long-form version with quote.
- Post 3 (IG): Player perspective. "40 players. Zero app store friction."
- Story: Behind the scenes of an event setup

### Week 4 — Drive Action
- Post 1 (IG): CTA-forward. "We're onboarding new tournaments for 2026."
- Post 2 (LI): "If you're organizing a golf event this year, this is for you." Direct pitch.
- Post 3 (IG): Testimonial if available. Otherwise: product showcase + link in bio.
- Story: FAQ series — "Can I use my own logo?" "What formats do you support?" "How fast is setup?"

## Metrics — What We Track

### 90-Day Goals
- Instagram: 500 followers, 3%+ engagement rate
- LinkedIn: 200 followers, 5 warm leads tagged by Jordan
- Direct traffic from social to marketing page: measurable (requires UTMs)

### What Matters
- Profile visits → link taps (Instagram) / post clicks (LinkedIn)
- DMs from organizers asking about pricing
- Jordan mentions social leads in pipeline

### What Doesn't Matter Right Now
- Impressions
- Reach
- Viral posts

We are not building an audience. We are building a pipeline. The metrics should reflect that.
