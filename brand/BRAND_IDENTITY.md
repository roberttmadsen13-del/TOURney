# TOURney — Brand Identity

> **Internal reference document. Governs all copy, design, and messaging decisions.**
> Owned by: Marketing / Sloane Hargrove
> Last updated: 2026-05-13

---

## Name

**TOURney** — always written with capital T and capital Y. No exceptions.

- Correct: TOURney
- Wrong: Tourney, tourney, TOURNEY, tourney, Tour-ney

The capitalization is deliberate. The TOUR is the sport. The ney is ours. That tension is the brand.

---

## Positioning Statement

**TOURney is the tournament platform for golf organizers who take the experience seriously — without taking themselves too seriously.**

We're not Golf Genius. We're not a spreadsheet with a domain name. We're the platform that makes a 40-person charity scramble feel like a real event — for the organizer, and for every player who installs the app to their home screen.

**One sentence:** Run your golf tournament like a pro.

---

## Target Personas

### Primary — The Organizer (who we serve)
- Club social chairperson. Corporate outing coordinator. Charity tournament director. Dad who runs the annual member-guest.
- Runs 1–3 events per year. Has done this on spreadsheets or a free tool that looked terrible.
- Pain point: the event matters to them. The tools they've used don't match that.
- Win condition: they look like a pro to their players. The app looks like *their* brand, not ours.

### Secondary — The Player (who they impress)
- Golfer who gets a link in a group text and opens it on their phone.
- Has never heard of TOURney. Sees a branded PWA with the tournament name and logo.
- Win condition: installs it to their home screen, checks the leaderboard obsessively, tells someone else how cool the scoring was.

### Tertiary — The Buyer (who pays)
- Same as the Organizer, but in the context of the purchasing decision.
- Concern: price, setup time, whether it will embarrass them in front of their players.
- Win condition: "$3/month. Up and running in 15 minutes. Less than a sleeve of balls."

## Brand Voice

### Tone Descriptors

| We are | We are not |
|--------|-----------|
| Confident | Arrogant |
| Direct | Blunt |
| Specific | Jargony |
| Premium | Stuffy |
| Self-aware | Self-deprecating |
| Warm | Casual |

### Voice Principles

**1. Outcomes over features.**
Don't tell them what it does. Tell them what they get.
- Wrong: "Live hole-by-hole scoring"
- Right: "Your players check the leaderboard between holes. Without you touching anything."

**2. The organizer is the hero. TOURney is the tool.**
We exist to make them look good. Every piece of copy should confirm that.
- Wrong: "TOURney's powerful admin panel lets you manage your tournament"
- Right: "Run the whole thing from your phone."

**3. Specificity is trust.**
Numbers. Real comparisons. Real time.
- Wrong: "Quick setup"
- Right: "Up and running in 15 minutes."

**4. Golf-literate without being golf-snobby.**
We know the difference between a scramble and a stableford. We don't explain it. We just demonstrate it.

**5. Dry wit is allowed. Emoji are not.**
"Less than a sleeve of balls" is brand voice. Emoji are not. The product is premium. Premium doesn't need reaction faces.

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Gold | `#c09030` | Primary brand color. Accents, CTAs, highlights. |
| Gold Light | `#d4a840` | Hover states, inline emphasis. |
| Ink | `#0a0703` | Primary background. Deep, rich, not pure black. |
| Cream | `#f5ede0` | Primary text on dark backgrounds. |
| Muted | `rgba(245,237,224,0.48)` | Secondary text, metadata, labels. |
| Green | `#27ae60` | Success states, live indicators. |

### Typography

| Role | Typeface | Usage |
|------|---------|-------|
| Display / Headlines | Barlow Condensed | Hero headlines, section headers, CTAs, nav brand. All-caps + letter-spacing where appropriate. |
| Functional / Mono | DM Mono | Sub-headlines, eyebrows, metadata, labels, pricing. Monospace signals precision. |
| Body | System sans (or Barlow) | Long-form copy, feature descriptions. |

### Design Principles

- Dark-first. The platform lives at night, at the turn, on a phone in a golf cart.
- Gold is earned. Used sparingly — it should feel like an accent, not a paint job.
- The branded PWA reflects the *tenant's* brand, not ours. TOURney is invisible to the player on purpose.

## Key Differentiators

Ordered by impact on conversion. Use these when positioning against competitors.

1. **Installable PWA — no app store.** Players add it to their home screen. Zero friction. Golf Genius doesn't do this.
2. **White-label by default.** Players see the tournament brand, never TOURney. The organizer gets credit.
3. **Admin from your phone.** No laptop required. Run the whole event from your pocket.
4. **Mixed format + handicap.** Scramble, stroke play, best ball, stableford, net scoring — in the same platform.
5. **Price.** Starts at $3/month. Under a sleeve of balls.

## What We Say / What We Never Say

### We say
- "Run your tournament like a pro."
- "Stop running your tournament on a spreadsheet."
- "Your players get a live-scoring PWA with your logo."
- "Up and running in 15 minutes."
- "Less than a sleeve of balls."
- "Your brand, not ours."

### We never say
- "Powerful" (every B2B SaaS says this)
- "Seamless" (meaningless)
- "Best-in-class" (who graded it)
- "Easy to use" (show, don't tell)
- "Tournament management solution" (it's a platform)
- "Bova Invitational" in product-facing copy (that's a tenant)
