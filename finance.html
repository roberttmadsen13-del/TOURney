Situation

Stripe integration is A-quality — edge functions, webhook, DB schema, and tier gating are all wired. The business scaffolding is pre-launch D: LLC not formed, no live Stripe keys, zero revenue, no ToS/Privacy Policy, no accounting. Phases 1–3 are hard blockers to first dollar. Phases 4–8 are operational hygiene that should run in parallel once Phase 1 unblocks.

Phase 1 — Legal Foundation
Form LLC + Obtain EIN P0 BLOCKER Rob Manual
Single-member LLC formation under "Greenskeeper Studios LLC". EIN via IRS Form SS-4 (online, free, same-day approval). LLC is prerequisite for business Stripe account, business bank, and personal liability protection. Operating as sole proprietor exposes all personal assets to business risk.
Acceptance Criteria
  • LLC articles filed with state + approval received
  • EIN issued by IRS (confirmation letter or online confirmation)
  • Single-member operating agreement drafted and signed
  • No personal assets mixed with business post-formation
Open Mercury Business Bank Account P0 Blocked on LLC + EIN Rob Manual
Mercury (mercury.com) is the recommended account for solo founders — no monthly fees, API access, strong Stripe integration. Requires LLC formation documents + EIN. Primary use: receive Stripe payouts, pay business expenses, document owner draws.
Acceptance Criteria
  • Mercury account open under Greenskeeper Studios LLC EIN
  • Debit card received
  • Stripe payouts directed to Mercury account
  • Zero personal transactions in this account
Phase 2 — ToS + Privacy Policy
Deploy Terms of Service + Privacy Policy P0 Required before live payments Rob Manual
Stripe requires ToS acceptance for live payments. Privacy Policy is legally required under CCPA/GDPR if collecting any user data. Recommended: Termly (~$10/mo) or Iubenda for generated policies. Both must be linked from footer across all pages.
Acceptance Criteria
  • /terms and /privacy routes live and accessible
  • Footer links to both on index.html, admin.html, player.html, marketing.html
  • ToS checkbox on registration flow before payment capture
  • Policies cover: data collection, payment processing, cancellation, contact info

Phase 4 — Accounting Setup

Set up chart of accounts for SaaS: Revenue, Stripe Fees (2.9%+30¢/txn), Hosting, AI API, Domain/Tools, Owner Draw.

  • First month reconciled with zero unmatched transactions

Phase 5 — Tax Posture
Quarterly Estimated Tax Payments P1
As sole member LLC (pass-through), Rob owes SE tax (~15.3%) + income tax on net profit. No withholding exists — quarterly estimates required to avoid underpayment penalty. 2026 remaining due dates: Jun 16, Sep 15, Jan 15, 2027. Rule of thumb: set aside 30% of every Stripe payout.
Acceptance Criteria
  • IRS account at irs.gov/account linked to EIN
  • Calendar reminders: Jun 16, Sep 15, Jan 15 (and Apr 15 2027)
  • Wave rule: tag every Stripe payout — 30% auto-allocated to "Tax Reserve" category
  • S-corp election decision documented: evaluate if annual profit exceeds $40k (breakeven point where SE tax savings exceed S-corp admin cost)
