Fix tenant flash — body visibility guard in tourney-init.js
Problem: Before tourney-init.js resolves the tournament data fetch, all tournament pages briefly show stale/default values — Bova's tournament name, colors, and hero content flash visible (~200–600ms).

Approach:
1. In tourney-init.js, at the very top of the IIFE, add: document.documentElement.style.visibility = 'hidden';
2. After injectBrandColors() resolves and all DOM updates are complete, add: document.documentElement.style.visibility = '';

This ensures the page only becomes visible after the correct tournament branding is applied. The transition is instant — no fade needed since tourney-init resolves in <200ms on a good connection. Add a safety timeout of 800ms to reveal anyway in case of slow network: setTimeout(() => document.documentElement.style.visibility = '', 800);
Verify Hard-reload /t/four-team-test (or any non-bova tenant). No flash of Bova content. Page appears directly with correct tournament branding.
